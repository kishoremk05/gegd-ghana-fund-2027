GEGD 2027 — Complete Project Concept & System Workflow
1. PROJECT IDENTITY
Project name

GEGD 2027 — Ghana Economic Growth & Development Week

Website

gegd.ghana-fund.com

Core concept

GEGD 2027 is not just an exhibition information website.

It is intended to be a commercial exhibition marketplace where businesses can:

Exhibit products
Reserve exhibition stands
Meet buyers
Find distributors
Secure orders
Find investors
Develop partnerships
Explore export opportunities
Participate in business meetings

18. CRITICAL PAYMENT RULE

This rule must be implemented exactly.

Paying $1,000 does NOT mean the stand is finally confirmed.

The client document explicitly states:

Payment of the US$1,000 non-refundable reservation fee does not constitute final stand allocation.

The stand is confirmed only after the full invoice has been paid and the payment has been verified by GEGD Secretariat.

Therefore:

Reservation Fee Paid
        ≠
Stand Confirmed

Instead:

Reservation Fee Paid
        ↓
Invoice
        ↓
Balance Paid
        ↓
GEGD Verification
        ↓
Stand Confirmed

You are developing GEGD 2027, Ghana Economic Growth & Development Week. This is not a simple landing-page redesign. The main frontend must use React. The website must be responsive across desktop, tablet and mobile. The design must be industrial, international, commercial, African and modern—not a carnival/festival design.

GEGD 2027 is the first edition. Never invent previous-event history or use fake previous GEGD photographs. Use artist impressions and properly licensed industrial/business imagery where required.

The supplied modular exhibition drawing is a technical reference. Do not simply embed it as the final floor plan. Redraw the final exhibition layout as an interactive SVG/web map. The floor plan must support zoom, pan, stand numbers, stand status, industry filtering, company search, stand details and stand selection. Stand availability must come from the database. Never allow two users to reserve the same stand.

The exhibitor booking process consists of company information, authorised contact information, exhibition information, package selection and interactive stand selection.

The payment process is two-stage. The first payment is a US$1,000 non-refundable reservation fee. Payment of this fee does NOT constitute final stand allocation. The final stand becomes CONFIRMED only after the full invoice has been paid and the GEGD Secretariat has verified the final payment. All outstanding invoices must be paid before 1 November 2026.

Do not tightly couple the application to Paystack because the client currently cannot use Paystack. Build provider-independent payment architecture. Initially support the client's available Mobile Money and Bank Transfer testing workflow, with manual verification where necessary. Future payment providers can be plugged in without rebuilding booking logic.

Create secure roles for ADMIN, EXHIBITOR, BUYER and VISITOR. Admin operations must be protected server-side. Payment status, stand availability and confirmation status must never be controlled only by the frontend.

The exhibitor dashboard must provide application status, stand, payment status, invoice, receipt, outstanding balance, payment deadline, exhibitor profile, product catalogue, buyer requests, meeting requests, exhibitor manual, technical manual, badges, event information and GEGD messages.

The platform must include an online exhibitor directory searchable by company, country, industry, product, service, export product, distributor opportunity and investment opportunity. Exhibitor profiles should support company information, products, stand number, website, contact, meeting requests and product-information requests.

The Buyer Programme must allow buyers to register independently of exhibitors, specify interests and find exhibitors/products for business meetings and opportunities. Visitor registration is separate and collects the fields specified in the GEGD requirements.

Create an administration/Secretariat system for managing exhibitors, applications, stands, stand numbers, stand types, prices, reservations, blocked stands, sponsor/pavilion allocations, stand status, invoices, payments, exhibitor approval, exhibitor movement and floor-plan data.

The exhibitor lifecycle is: DRAFT → APPLICATION SUBMITTED → RESERVATION FEE PENDING → RESERVATION FEE PAID → INVOICE ISSUED → BALANCE PENDING → FULLY PAID → CONFIRMED, with CANCELLED as an alternative state.

Implement automated email events for application received, reservation-fee invoice, reservation-fee received, balance invoice, payment reminder, final payment received, stand confirmation, manual issued, exhibitor profile approved, buyer meeting request and event reminder.
